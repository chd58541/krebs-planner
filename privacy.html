Krebs planner privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

Krebs planner is a private app used by one family on their own computer. It has no other users.

What the app accesses

With the family's permission, the app uses Google Calendar to view, create, change and delete events on the family's shared calendar. It does not use other calendars or other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

Calendar events are shown in the app and are not kept as a separate copy. If a family member links an event to a planned expense, that event's title, date and ID are saved in the family's own plan file. The Google sign-in key is stored on the family's computer.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. The app runs no analytics or tracking.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be ended at any time with the Disconnect button in the app, or at myaccount.google.com/connections.
